How to read this

  • Overview: check total threads and deadlocks.
  • By State: many WAITING or BLOCKED threads can indicate contention.
  • Thread States (Chart): visual representation of thread distribution across different states.
  • AI Insights: AI-powered analysis providing intelligent observations about the thread dump, including potential issues and patterns.
  • Root Cause Analysis: Detailed investigation of the primary causes of thread issues, with supporting evidence and recommended actions.
  • Hot Methods: top frames that appear most often — hotspots.
  • Contention Suspects: locks with many waiters; owner thread shows who holds it.
  • Threads: rows tinted red/orange are most relevant; click View under the name for full details and stack.
